ERC3 September 2026

ERC Starting Grant for muscle regeneration

We were awarded a Starting Grant by the European Research Council to advance ultrasound-based biofabrication for muscle regeneration.

The project will develop biocompatible soft materials that crosslink where ultrasound waves overlap, together with the ultrasound device needed to form precise tissue scaffolds. The goal is to enable tissue engineering directly inside the body.

Read the ETH Zurich announcement →
ARDF17 August 2026

ARDF grant for engineered muscle models

We were awarded a grant by the Alternatives Research & Development Foundation through its 2026 Annual Open Grant programme.

The funded project, “Engineered tissue models of skeletal muscle disease in an automated platform for high throughput drug toxicology,” supports the development of non-animal methods for biomedical research and testing. Funding begins on 1 October 2026.
